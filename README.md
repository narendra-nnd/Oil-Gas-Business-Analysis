# Oil-Gas-Business-Analysis
# 🛢️ Oil & Gas Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Oil & Gas Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of production, revenue, profitability, regional performance, product-wise analysis, operational downtime, and safety performance.

The dashboard is designed to help analyze oil and gas operations across different **regions, fields, operators, products, well types, and extraction methods**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall oil and gas production performance
* Track revenue and profitability
* Identify regional and field-level performance
* Analyze production trends over time
* Compare different product and well types
* Monitor operational downtime
* Analyze safety incidents and incident rates
* Provide an interactive platform for data-driven analysis

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI dashboard consists of **5 analytical pages**.

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the overall operation through key performance indicators and visualizations.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Revenue
* Net Profit
* Total Production
* Incident Rate
* Total Downtime Hours

**Visualizations include:**

* Total Revenue by Month
* Profit Margin
* Net Profit by Region
* Total Production by Product Type

---

### 2. Production Trend

This page focuses on analyzing operational performance over time.

**Key analyses:**

* Total Production Cost by Month
* Profit Margin by Month
* Total Downtime Hours by Month

These visualizations help identify changes in production costs, profitability, and downtime patterns.

---

### 3. Regional Analysis

This page analyzes performance across different geographical regions and fields.

**Key analyses:**

* Profit Margin by Region
* Total OpEx by Field
* Total Revenue and Production Cost by Region

This helps identify regions and fields with different cost, revenue, and profitability characteristics.

---

### 4. Product Analysis

This page focuses on production characteristics and extraction methods.

**Key analyses:**

* Net Profit by Extraction Method
* Total Production by Well Type
* Total Revenue and Net Profit by Product Type

The dashboard compares conventional, hydraulic fracturing, EOR, thermal, and offshore extraction methods, along with vertical, horizontal, directional, and multilateral wells.

---

### 5. Safety Compliance

This page focuses on operational safety and incident analysis.

**Key analyses:**

* Incident Rate by Field
* Total Safety Incidents by Safety Rating
* Incident Rate by Operator

Safety incidents are analyzed across different severity levels and operational entities.

1. Download or clone this repository.

3. Open the `.pbix` dashboard file.
4. Connect the dashboard to the required dataset.
5. Refresh the data.
6. Use the available filters and slicers to explore the analysis.
